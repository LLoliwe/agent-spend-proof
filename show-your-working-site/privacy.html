Privacy & website information

Show Your Working is a research-stage education technology project developed by Pryntor (Pty) Ltd, Durban, South Africa. This informational website does not offer live grading, student file upload, accounts, forms, payments or subscriptions.

Information and contact

The static source of this site does not set marketing cookies or include custom analytics. Our hosting provider may retain standard technical access logs, which are governed by the provider's own policies. If you email us, we process the information you voluntarily share to respond to your enquiry. Do not email identifiable learner records or confidential scripts.

Research status

No public classroom product or learner data collection is operated here. Independent educational review, appropriate consent and privacy assessment are prerequisites for any later learner-facing research.
